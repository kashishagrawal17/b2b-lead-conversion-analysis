# B2B Lead Conversion Analysis
### Project Overview
This project analyses the lead conversion journey of a B2B shipping platform that helps small merchants ship their products.

The goal was to understand why some leads convert while others drop off, identify bottlenecks in the sales process, evaluate marketing performance, and suggest practical improvements to increase conversions.

The analysis was performed on 100 leads along with call activity data to study lead quality, response time, funnel movement, and ownership tracking.

### Business Problem
The company receives leads from multiple marketing channels. These leads are first qualified by the Filtration team and then transferred to Sales for conversion.

The key questions explored were:

- How many leads are converting?
- Where are leads getting stuck?
- Which marketing sources bring better customers?
- Does faster response time improve conversion?
- Are qualified leads being properly tracked after handoff?
  
### Key Insights
The overall conversion rate was 16% across 100 leads.
Not Connected was the biggest funnel challenge, with 16 leads stuck at this stage.
Organic leads performed best, generating 12 conversions from 41 leads (29.3%).
Faster response had a positive impact, with leads contacted within 15 minutes converting at 22.7% compared to 9.5% after 30 minutes.
A potential ownership tracking gap was identified, as most converted leads were still assigned to Filtration owners.

### Recommendations
Based on the analysis, the company can improve conversions by:
- Reducing lead response time with a clear first-call target.
- Improving follow-up processes for unreachable leads.
- Reviewing low-performing marketing channels.
- Strengthening CRM ownership tracking after lead qualification.

### Conclusion
This project highlights how data analysis can uncover hidden gaps in a sales funnel. By improving response speed, lead management, and marketing efficiency, the company can increase conversions without only focusing on generating more leads.
